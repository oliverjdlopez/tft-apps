### Question

### Concrete analytical scenario

### Reference requirements translated to ChatTFT

Select ordinary triplets through the local item catalogue and resolved identities. Use compare_cohorts on explicit holder-bound triplets within shared observed companion/frontline conditions and final levels. Enforce one Aphelios copy for joint item predicates. Use query_cohort for board item-count and support composition diagnostics.

### Metrics and acceptance checks

Keep both-items separate, preserve overlap/suppression/interval warnings, and do not substitute item marginals. Without a common pair, give descriptive whole-build results and say the one-slot contrast is unresolved.

### Capability limits and preparation

Exact global item budget, all ordinary equipment on the entire board and automatic matching are unavailable; ordinary here refers to the selected Aphelios triplet. Declare residual companion investment differences. Historical 901-game web evidence supplies no current gold value.
