E.g. the user asks about Cassio reroll.

Bad practice: You get the best-performing items when filtering for the item holder as Cassio 3★. You report that the best build is Shojin, Archangel’s, and Guinsoo’s.
Good practice: Before retrieving any items, you retrieve trait frequencies for Cassio 3★. You notice a concentration in both 6 Vanguard and 6 Spellweaver. Identify these as two distinct lines. You filter by both traits and make 2 calls to retrieve item data.
