# Mechanic-Specific Items

Set 18, Enchanted Wilds, patch 18.1.

## Wisp potions

- **Blast Potion, Health Potion, Mana Potion** — temporary equippable items supplied by their respective Wisps; upgraded potion records specify Radiant versions. **Potioncraft** supplies a potion bundle.

## Permanent stat consumables

- **Doodad Jar / Bag / Sack** — consumable defensive-stat rewards: Armor/MR or Health. Their stat gains are permanent.
- **Knick-Knack Jar / Bag / Sack** — consumable AD or AS rewards with permanent stat gains.
- **Thingamajig Jar / Bag / Sack** — consumable AP or Mana Regen rewards with permanent stat gains.

## Trait selectors

- **Alpha Mark** — chooses the Riftbeast that receives its unique Alpha buff. Omega Riftbeast allows a second choice.
- **Sprykin rider selection** — dropping a Sprykin onto the BFF selects its rider; an Emblem can give a different unit the Sprykin trait.
- **Emerald Aspect pairing** — dragging an ally onto the strongest Taric chooses the recipient of his paired-ally ability benefits.
