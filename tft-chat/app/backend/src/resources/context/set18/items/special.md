# Emblems and Special Items

Set 18, Enchanted Wilds, patch 18.1.

## Emblems

- **Fae Emblem** — adds Fae to its holder.
- **Hunter Emblem** — adds Hunter to its holder; takedowns add AD.
- **Inferno Emblem** — adds Inferno to its holder.
- **Rapidfire Emblem** — adds Rapidfire to its holder; attacks add target-max-Health true damage.
- **Elderwood Emblem** — adds Elderwood to its holder.
- **Ravager Emblem** — adds Ravager to its holder; Health restored accumulates Damage Amp.
- **Sprykin Emblem** — adds Sprykin to its holder; riding the BFF adds AS and resists.
- **Vanguard Emblem** — adds Vanguard to its holder; surviving the combat timer restores player Health.
- **Blossom Emblem** — adds Blossom to its holder.
- **Spellweaver Emblem** — adds Spellweaver to its holder; allied casts grant Mana.
- **Invoker Emblem** — adds Invoker to its holder; casting grants AP based on Mana spent.
- **Lunar Emblem** — adds Lunar to its holder.
- **Brawler Emblem** — adds Brawler to its holder; attacks add holder-max-Health magic damage.
- **Blackthorn Emblem** — adds Blackthorn to its holder.
- **Executioner Emblem** — adds Executioner to its holder; damage executes sufficiently injured enemies.
- **Primal Emblem** — adds Primal to its holder.
- **Coven Emblem** — adds Coven to its holder.
- **Defender Emblem** — adds Defender to its holder; opening AS scales with front-row allies.
- **Flora Fatalis Emblem** — adds Flora Fatalis to its holder.
- **Juggernaut Emblem** — adds Juggernaut to its holder; Juggernaut deaths grant Mana.

## Augment and Wisp equipment variants

- **Consuming Flora emblem** — the augment's Flora Fatalis Emblem doubles its holder's trait benefits; this is distinct from simply granting Flora Fatalis.
- **Radiant Lucky Item Chest** — offers equipment matched to the chosen champion's recommended items.
- **Phantom Emblem** — Wisp-created emblem for the most active trait; temporary.
- **Phantom Armor / Phantom Vest / Phantom Gloves** — temporary Warmog, Bramble or Thief's Gloves equipment respectively; prismatic variants can change quantity and quality.

## Components and recipe families

- **B.F. Sword, Recurve Bow, Needlessly Large Rod, Tear of the Goddess, Chain Vest, Negatron Cloak, Giant's Belt, Sparring Gloves** — basic components used by the completed-item recipes in the live catalogue.
- **Spatula and Frying Pan** — special recipe components for trait/team-size equipment. Cooking Pot and U.R.F explicitly interact with items built from them.
