# Combat and Horizontal Traits

Set 18, Enchanted Wilds, patch 18.1.

- **Adaptor** — (2/3/4) changes each Adaptor's ability with its higher AD or AP and supplies the higher offensive stat. The listed default form is not a permanent role restriction.
- **Brawler** — (2/4/6) grants flat team Health and additional percentage Health to Brawlers.
- **Defender** — (2/4/6) grants team Armor and MR, with larger bonuses for Defenders.
- **Executioner** — (2/3/4) supplies Precision and critical chance, then adds a true-damage bleed and strengthens it. Precision enables ability critical strikes.
- **Hunter** — (2/3/4/5) grants AD; staying on one target for four seconds gives a Hunter Damage Amp.
- **Invoker** — (2/3/4/5) supplies team Mana Regen, with a larger amount for Invokers.
- **Juggernaut** — (2/4/6) supplies team Durability, with a larger amount for Juggernauts.
- **Rapidfire** — (2/3/4/5) supplies team Attack Speed; Rapidfire attacks build additional AS up to ten stacks.
- **Ravager** — (2/4/6) supplies Omnivamp and bonus damage; the damage bonus doubles against targets below half Health.
- **Spellweaver** — (2/4/6) supplies team AP, extra AP for Spellweavers, and further AP when a Spellweaver casts.
- **Summoner** — (2/3) improves Yorick's summon Health, Azir and Mama Beak summon damage, and Zyra plant attacks. Three Summoners strengthens each benefit by half.
- **Vanguard** — (2/4/6) supplies a max-Health shield at combat start and on dropping below half Health. At 6, also grants Durability while shielded.
