# Major and Vertical Traits

Set 18, Enchanted Wilds, patch 18.1.

- **Blackthorn** — (2/4/6) sacrifices the unit on its special hex before combat, grants team Health, and gives Blackthorn units extra stats determined by the sacrifice's role, cost and stars. Team Health bonuses are 175/300/550 team Health, with a 60% stronger bonus at 4 and 6.
- **Blossom** — (3/5/7/9/11) empowers Wisps after combat and grants Blossom units AD, AP and max Health. Milestones upgrade Wisps, offer them every shop, refund gold on purchase, permit two purchases per round, and unlock the prismatic empowerment.
- **Coven** — (3/4/5/7) collects Essence from kills and lost player combats; at reward thresholds, cash out or continue collecting. Initial decision threshold: 40 Essence. Kill/loss Essence is 2/18, 2/32, 3/32, or 10/60 by breakpoint.
- **Elderwood** — (3/5/7/9/11) supplies placeable plants: Stonebark Tree plus Lifebloom; second Stonebark; Deepwood Protector; two-star plants; three-star plants. Plant Health and AP scale with total Elderwood stars.
- **Fae** — (2/4) attracts Pixies through team damage, healing and shielding. Pixies grant Fae AD/AP and a heal when they fall below half Health; at 4 Fae, collecting seven Pixies unlocks gold-producing Golden Pixies.
- **Inferno** — (2/3/5/7) applies Burn and Wound; its Burn can stack with other Burn sources. At 3/5/7, upgrades 1/2/4 non-Inferno shop slots after combat by one cost tier.
- **Lunar** — (2/3/4/5) provides AP and Attack Speed to Lunar units and adjacent allies; Lunar units receive twice the bonus. Three Lunar plus three Solar activates Eclipse.
- **Primal** — (2/4) offers a choice of four Primal Blessings. Blessing-specific effects are separate from the generic trait; the Beast Within augment grants an additional choice.
- **Riftbeast** — (3/5/7/10) grants an Alpha Mark, then a Riftbeast shop every three combats, then recurring combat stat growth, then +2 team size. Patch 18.1's August 31 update sets the AD/AP/AS growth at 7 Riftbeast to 5% each.
- **Solar** — (3) grants a team shield and bonus magic damage. Unique three-star units improve its bonuses; higher three-star milestones add stats, true-damage conversion and eventual four-star ascensions. Three Solar plus three Lunar activates Eclipse.
- **Sprykin** — (3/5/7) summons the Big Furry Friend (BFF). Placing a Sprykin onto it chooses the rider, whose Health and Attack Speed increase. BFF attacks and ability depend on whether the rider is melee or ranged; higher breakpoints extend its ability benefit to Sprykin allies.
- **Eclipse** — requires both 3 Solar and 3 Lunar. Beginning 10 seconds into combat, kills the lowest-Health enemy, then repeats every 3.5 seconds.
