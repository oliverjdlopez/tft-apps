## UN4-2-C01 — AP-oriented Nidalee within comparable boards

### Question

Are AP builds on two-star Nidalee competitive with AD builds, or do they only hold up on certain boards?

### Concrete analytical scenario

Nidalee2 with exactly three ordinary items, partitioning explicit AP-oriented, AD-oriented and mixed triplets; compare within observed cores and Invoker strata.

### Reference requirements translated to ChatTFT

rank_unit_loadouts discovers actual exact trios; use the item catalogue to declare a reproducible orientation taxonomy. compare_cohorts contrasts named triplets within shared supports and trait contribution intervals. query_cohort/deltas discovers the allied package, with entity names retained in grouped rows.

### Metrics and acceptance checks

Keep mixed/ambiguous trios separate and show support/outcomes for each compared package. Investigate whether differences persist inside named contexts, not only aggregate AP/AD labels. No fixed preferred build or ranking is expected.

### Capability limits and preparation

No AP/AD filter or observed Adaptor-form field exists. A named item-based orientation proxy must be explicit and must not be called confirmed ranged/melee form. Other inventory and global investment remain residual confounders.
