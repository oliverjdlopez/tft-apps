## TR-3-C01 — Defender Cassiopeia: competing emblem holders

### Question

In Defender Cassiopeia boards, do Rengar or Kha’Zix versions actually finish better when they hold the Defender emblem?

### Concrete analytical scenario

Cassiopeia carry with Fiddlesticks and six-Defender support, comparing Defender Emblem on Rengar versus Kha'Zix with explicit holder stars and remaining two item slots.

### Reference requirements translated to ChatTFT

Use exact holder-bound emblem predicates, named one-copy holder guards and carry/frontline investment in compare_cohorts. Discover actual supporting cores and verify Defender contribution counts; inspect alternate cores rather than treating six Defender as a roster.

### Metrics and acceptance checks

Sample sizes and mean/top4/win must come from the joint emblem-holder/core cells. Preserve holder upgrades and remaining items, report support-slot changes and both-holder overlap if present. An emblem-wide ranking is insufficient.

### Capability limits and preparation

Exactly one Defender emblem can be counted by its exact item name; zero other emblem families/global upgraded-board cost cannot be assumed. Rival choices and unseen trait grants are not recoverable from generic unit names.
