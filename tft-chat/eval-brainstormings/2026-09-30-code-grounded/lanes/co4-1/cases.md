## CO4-1-C01 — An AP repertoire with ordinary resources

### Question

What two or three AP comps are worth learning on 18.3B without relying on an artifact, emblem, or a big reroll high roll, and what core units and item needs distinguish them?

### Concrete analytical scenario

An AP learning repertoire for a verified 18.3B scope. Start with ordinary-item named carry builds and avoid relying on multiple three-star supports; Ahri/Soraka/Zyra are discovery leads rather than a closed eligible pool.

### Reference requirements translated to ChatTFT

Use rank_units and rank_unit_loadouts to identify reportable AP-oriented candidates from context, then cohort unit/trait discovery and conjunction checks to define recurring support packages. Compare reportable named build/star/level cohorts and inspect global equipment distributions with query_cohort. State the candidate-pool coverage and what no-high-roll proxy was actually checked.

### Metrics and acceptance checks

Provide two or three substantively different supported packages with core, item-holder needs, counts and mean/top4/win. Popularity alone is insufficient. Do not select only winning boards or equate final three-stars with luck. Missing eligible candidates/pages limits completeness.

### Capability limits and preparation

No composition-cluster tool, generic AP filter, boardwide artifact/emblem exclusion or global three-star-count filter is exposed. A strictly ordinary whole-board/no-multiple-three-star benchmark needs a prepared population; current-tool results must be labeled ordinary carry builds with inspected support, not falsely certified full-resource eligibility.

### Reference answer contract

Answer the player question from actual scoped results, identifying the observed packages and direction or unresolved comparison. Report the case-specific counts and outcome tradeoffs, and distinguish an unsupported estimate from zero. A legitimate supported result may favor either alternative. Historical provisional answers and online ranking numbers are not the benchmark's expected values.

## CO4-1-C02 — Does Veigar belong in a top-four repertoire?

### Question

For an ordinary-item AP repertoire on 18.3B, should Veigar–Ornn be on my list if I care more about top fours than firsts, or is its good placement mostly coming from specialist-item boards?

### Concrete analytical scenario

Veigar–Ornn ordinary versus specialist-item versions, with Veigar reroll permitted and top-four priority; compare a separately defined ordinary Zyra–Amumu package as repertoire context.

### Reference requirements translated to ChatTFT

Discover Veigar loadouts and named Ornn/carry support cells, partition explicit holder trios and named special-item conditions, and compare within shared level/star/core strata. Use returned comparison histograms to count top-four outcomes for disjoint reportable categories; compare Zyra–Amumu as a separate package, not a matched Veigar swap.

### Metrics and acceptance checks

Report both group top4_rate and contribution to the family's observed top fours, with the same denominator and nonoverlapping category definition. Include win-rate tradeoff and sample sizes. A higher artifact-group rate does not mean it supplies most successes.

### Capability limits and preparation

Ordinary items on Veigar do not imply ordinary items across the board. Generic special-item-count predicates are absent, so whole-board partition/share claims need preparation or must remain explicitly incomplete. Do not sum overlapping special cohorts or multiply rounded rates into exact counts.

Azir3 end states partitioned by his completed-item investment and named teammate investment: primary-carry proxy, co-carry proxy, support proxy and ambiguous. Include a three-item Azir/zero-item Kha'Zix sensitivity.
