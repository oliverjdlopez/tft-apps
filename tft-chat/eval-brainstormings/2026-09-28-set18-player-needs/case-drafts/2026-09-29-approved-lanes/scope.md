# Scope and evidence conventions

This collection contains candidate investigations for intermediate Teamfight Tactics players using Set 18 standard-ranked data. It focuses on asynchronous analysis of completed endgame boards: item builds and holders, units and partners, composition families, and trait packages. Questions should require comparison, synthesis, or discovery from relevant board data; basic mechanics, static roster lookups, trait arithmetic, positioning, transition routes, and live-game spot optimization are outside this collection. Questions use natural player language and may leave analytical filters in the case notes.

Cases may adapt a broad player discussion into a more specific comparison or synthesize a useful question where the exact wording was not observed. Their origin labels and source notes preserve that distinction. A discussion motivates a need; it does not prove the answer. Rules references establish legal Set 18 entities and mechanics, while scoped outcome evidence supports statistical comparisons. Final-board data describes associations among completed boards. It does not by itself establish purchase or transition history, player intent, combat sequence, or causation.

Answers state what their cited evidence supports and identify unresolved decisive comparisons. Provisional and disputed cases can have useful partial answers; missing evidence is recorded plainly without inventing rankings or turning every question into an abstention exercise. Statistical claims require a compatible ranked population and observed patch/subpatch, period, region or rank scope, filters, metrics, and sample sizes where available. Separate windows or unlike cohorts are not pooled as if matched. The two supported answers are scoped descriptive snapshots; no fully matched current gameplay recommendation has been validated.

Each case's `source_ids` resolve to its lane's `sources.json`, which records access, provenance, observations, and limitations. Case-level status, answer status, comparison definition, lineage, and unresolved evidence are indexed in `case-index.json`. The approved question corresponding to every R2 lineage ID is retained in `proposal-lineage.json`.
