### Question

### Sample answer

I’m interpreting this as boards playing **both Soraka and Zyra**, rather than pooling separate carry compositions. The retrieved evidence does not settle whether Lillia holds up in that shell. The public [Lillia page](https://tactics.tools/units/da_18_lillia) shows a 4.18 average for two-star Lillia; the [Malphite page](https://tactics.tools/units/da_18_malphite) shows 4.30 at two stars on a different patch label. Those are whole-unit populations covering other carries and frontline roles. They cannot establish a Lillia advantage or parity here.

The unfinished part is identifying comparable Soraka/Zyra configurations that actually succeed with each tank. A successful Lillia build with different supporting traits would establish that package’s viability, not the result of replacing Malphite on a fixed board. I do not yet have the shared-shell counts and placements needed to tell you where Lillia holds up.

Juggernaut Elder Dragon, including Draven and Maokai, shows 4.72, 45.9% and 18.1% over 2.3M.

Those are different packages: the displayed Ezreal version also gives Draven an Executioner Emblem, and supporting rosters differ. Common patch, rank, region and collection period are not exposed. Template stars are not verified sample filters. Therefore these rows do **not** establish Ezreal as the better secondary at equal investment.

The [Draven guide](https://tftflow.com/composition/set18/draven-fast-9) recognizes both alternatives. Their relative top-four and win performance in comparable completed boards, especially without the emblem difference, remains unresolved.
