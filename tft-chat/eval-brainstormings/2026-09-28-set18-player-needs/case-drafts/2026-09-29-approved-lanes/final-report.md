## Supported conclusions and limits

CO4-4-C03 distinguishes the most popular level-nine Primal finish from the better displayed outcomes. CO4-4-C04 describes a small lead among level-ten Elder Dragon caps without treating it as a conclusive universal ranking. Both retain the inspected snapshot, shared roster, approximate counts and unresolved window/selection scope. Neither supplies a causal swap recommendation or a probability of winning after deciding to pursue a board.

Other cases use actual named-build rows, holder marginals and composition variations as partial evidence. These do not establish the exact build, star, carry-role, frontline or trait-package intersections asked about. All case files retain their original questions, sample answers, required data and annotations; each lane's source record preserves the evidence and its limits.

## Evidence gaps

No common patch/subpatch, date range, ranked population, rank/region scope or denominator was established across the public tables. The 18.3B plus September 28 rules reference is not a statistical dataset definition. Historical 18.2 observations stay historical. Header updates, last-14-days panels, shorter methodology windows and bracket totals cannot be silently pooled; overlapping variation rows are not mutually exclusive cohorts.

MetaTFT pages did not yield usable joint queries. Tactics.tools exposed references and selected broad rows while exact queries and scope metadata remained incomplete. Little Buddy Bot's relevant raster tables were unreadable. Other resources provided additional actual observations with their own restrictions. The retained lane source records identify the specific access limits that affect each case; none is a claim that the needed data do not exist.

Several prerequisites require special care. CO4-4-C02 lacks the sixth common unit from an unreadable image. UN4-2-C04 needs double-Blue inventory legality verified. Lux and Nidalee identities must not be inferred from ambiguous labels or route suffixes. Alpha Mark, Primal blessing, sacrifices, permanent stacks and artifact partners remain unobserved unless explicitly recorded. These constraints limit explanations while preserving useful descriptive questions.
