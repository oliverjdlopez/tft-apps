## TR-3-C01 — Defender Cassiopeia: competing emblem holders

### Question

In Defender Cassiopeia boards, do Rengar or Kha’Zix versions actually finish better when they hold the Defender emblem?

### Sample answer

Neither holder has a demonstrated advantage in the evidence collected. [TFT Flow](https://tftflow.com/composition/set18/defender-cassiopeia) lists both variants, while older comments criticize Kha’Zix even after upgrades. A [separate discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/CompetitiveTFT/comments/1w57056/even_if_i_read_and_watch_guides_i_cant_seem_to/) describes Rengar replacing Leona or Ornn. These are alternatives worth testing, but neither a guide tier nor an isolated eighth establishes which finishes better. The head-to-head conclusion remains unfinished, particularly because [18.3 changed relevant units and traits](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/teamfight-tactics-patch-18-3/).

## TR-3-C02 — Hunter Camille and Caitlyn as an alternative

### Question

Is Hunter-emblem Camille with Caitlyn a real alternative to Nidalee/Sivir, or does that board mostly fall short?

### Sample answer

Older holder statistics lean against Camille as a broad substitute, but do not settle this particular board. [TopMeta’s opened 18.2 table](https://topmeta.gg/en/items/hunter-emblem/) reports Camille at 5.45 average placement, 31.6% top four and 5.4% first across 6.9K records; Nidalee is 4.08, 59.0% and 12.7% across 163K. These are unmatched champion rows, with no Caitlyn/Sivir or star restriction. They cannot rule out an upgraded Camille/Caitlyn niche, and [18.3 changes](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/teamfight-tactics-patch-18-3/) prevent treating them as current verdicts. The specific alternative’s answer remains unfinished.

## TR-3-C04 — Brawler Yi without artifact dependence

There is evidence of an ordinary-item Brawler Yi build, so the idea is not restricted to an artifact on Yi himself. [Noxu’s named build row](https://noxutft.com/tft-comps/adaptor-master-yi) pairs the emblem with Edge of Night and Guinsoo’s Rageblade and reports 4.25 average placement over 1.7k records. But it does not exclude artifacts elsewhere or establish top-four reliability for that exact build. Broad composition totals also differ between the detail and index pages. This is a useful starting signal, not proof that the emblem alone reproduces artifact-board performance. The comparison remains unfinished.

## TR-3-C05 — Inferno Kha’Zix and the freed Akali slot

### Question

In five-Inferno Kha’Zix endgames, does putting Inferno emblem on Kha’Zix and replacing Akali actually improve the final board?

### Sample answer

The emblem creates a concrete roster alternative: the [opened suggestion](https://www.reddit.com/r/TeamfightTactics/comments/1winnef/whats_your_hear_me_out_comp_this_patch/) removes Akali while retaining the five-Inferno package around Kha’Zix. But a free unit slot is purchased with one of Kha’Zix’s item slots. No retrieved outcome table prices both changes together, so the improvement is still unproven. The stronger final board may depend on the replacement and the item the emblem displaced; naming either a winner now would overstate the evidence.
